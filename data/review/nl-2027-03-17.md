# Netherlands legal check, 27 October 2026

This note is not legal advice. It is the editorial check for a national hub and the Provincial States and water-board elections of 17 March 2027. Germany, France, Japan, South Korea and Türkiye stay off the site.

## Date and hours

Read on 27 October 2026:

- Kiesraad, Provincial States elections (`https://www.kiesraad.nl/verkiezingen/provinciale-staten`). The next Provincial States elections are on 17 March 2027. The seat table on that page is labelled as the 2023 overview. It was not copied as a 2027 allocation. The page does not print the opening or closing clock.
- Kiesraad election calendar of 24 August 2026, PDF (`https://www.kiesraad.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/08/24/verkiezingskalender-ps-ws27/verkiezingskalender-2027-psws27.pdf`). The calendar is for Provincial States and water boards on Wednesday 17 March 2027. Nomination day is Monday 1 February 2027. The count at a polling station starts immediately after voting, and not before 21:00.
- Elections Act (Kieswet), consolidation valid from 1 October 2026 (`https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004627/2026-10-01/0/AfdelingII/HoofdstukJ/Paragraaf1/ArtikelJ1`). Article J 1(2) says voting starts at 07:30 and lasts until 21:00. Article J 1a lets a municipal executive open a station earlier or later than 07:30 and close it earlier than 21:00. The count at those stations is not before 21:00. The count on this site stays off until 21:00, Central European Time (UTC+1), on 17 March 2027. Daylight-saving time in 2027 begins on the last Sunday of March, which is after this election day. The Act text that was read does not name a time zone.

## Surveys

A search of that Kieswet consolidation found no article on publishing opinion polls or exit polls. This site still publishes no poll and no projection. That is a choice of the site, not a blackout copied from a statute.

## Publisher and privacy

The page is an unofficial factual guide. It does not encourage a vote for a list. No media statute was read article by article. The Netherlands is in the European Union, so the GDPR applies. The Dutch implementation act was not section-read. The hub publishes no roll and no citizen-service number.

## Candidates

## Verdict

Publish the hub and the election pages. Provincial States and water boards on the same day. No municipality pages. No poll. No projection. No count before 21:00 Central European Time on 17 March 2027. The same-origin file `public/live/nl-2027-03-17.json` stays empty until then.
