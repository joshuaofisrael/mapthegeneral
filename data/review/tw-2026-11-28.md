# Taiwan local elections, 28 November 2026

Read on 27 October 2026. This is a source note for the page, not legal advice.

## Verdict

Publish a Traditional Chinese hub and election page, with an English switch. Print no survey figures and no candidate names. Do not print a count before 23:59 Taiwan time on 28 November 2026. That minute is the end of the voting day, not a closing time the Commission printed.

## What was read

- Legislative Yuan file of the Central Election Commission chair’s business report, 27 May 2026: https://ppg.ly.gov.tw/ppg/SittingAttachment/download/2026052199/0256000260722042002.pdf — the 622nd meeting on 31 October 2025 set voting for Saturday 28 November 2026 (ROC year 115). The timetable in that report includes the notice, registration, draw, announcement, voting-and-counting, and winner-announcement dates. It does not print opening or closing clock times.
- Public Officials Election and Recall Act article 53: https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?FLNO=53&Pcode=D0020010 — from ten days before voting day until voting ends, no one may publish, report, distribute, comment on, or quote the survey material described in the article.
- Central Election Commission interpretation, 21 December 2011: https://law.cec.gov.tw/LawContent.aspx?id=GL000134 — publishing that material in print, on the internet, on radio, or on television is covered.
- https://web.cec.gov.tw/central/article/61722 was not retrieved as article body. It is cited only as the Commission’s own news URL, not as a second source of the date.

## Rules applied

- The ten-day limit starts at 00:00 on 18 November 2026, Taiwan time. Because the closing clock time was not read, the page’s own gate stays shut until 23:59 on 28 November. The page says that 23:59 is not an official close.
- This site prints no survey figures on any day, including before 18 November.
- The Personal Data Protection Act was not section-read. The hub publishes no roll.

## Gaps

- Voting hours were not on the report. They are not guessed.
- Candidate announcements in the timetable are 12 and 17 November 2026, both after this note. No list was opened. The candidate file is empty.
- No 2026 results path was read. The fallback link is the Commission’s site, and the same-origin file starts empty.
- Germany, France, Japan, South Korea and Turkey stay excluded.
