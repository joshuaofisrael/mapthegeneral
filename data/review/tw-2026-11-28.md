# Taiwan legal check, 27 October 2026

This note is not legal advice. It is the editorial check for a national hub and the local public-office elections on Saturday 28 November 2026. Germany, France, Japan, South Korea and Türkiye stay off the site.

## Date

Read on 27 October 2026: the Central Election Commission’s report to the Legislative Yuan, dated ROC 115年7月16日 (16 July 2026), at `https://ppg.ly.gov.tw/ppg/SittingAttachment/download/2026071421/68105040273002713004.pdf`.

The report says the Commission’s 622nd meeting on ROC 114年10月31日 (31 October 2025) set voting for ROC 115年11月28日, a Saturday, which is 28 November 2026. The timetable in that report has no opening hour and no closing hour. The count on this site stays off until 00:00 on 29 November 2026, Taiwan time. That instant is the start of the next calendar day. It is not a closing time the report printed.

The same timetable sets a public notice of special-municipality mayoral candidates for 12 November 2026 and a public notice of special-municipality councillors, county and city mayors, and county and city councillors for 17 November 2026. No name list was in the report. The candidate file stays empty.

## Silence and surveys

Read on 27 October 2026:

- Article 53 of the Public Officials Election and Recall Act, `https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?FLNO=53&Pcode=D0020010`. From the election notice until ten days before voting day, published survey material must state the survey body, the host, the dates, the sampling method, the population, the sample size, the error, and the funding. From ten days before voting day until voting time ends, nobody may publish, report, distribute, comment on, or quote that material in any way.
- Article 5 of the same Act, `https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?FLNO=5&Pcode=D0020010`. A period described as a number of days before voting day is counted by starting on the day before voting day and counting backwards. Ten days before 28 November 2026 is 18 November 2026.

This site prints no survey figure and no projection on any day, including before 18 November. A count is not shown during that blackout. The same-origin file stays empty until 00:00 on 29 November 2026, Taiwan time.

## Publisher

The page is an unofficial factual guide. It does not encourage a vote for a candidate. No separate media-licensing statute was read article by article. Article 53 already covers any person. The identification rules in the first paragraph of that article are not triggered, because no survey is published. That unread licensing statute is a gap, not a reason to skip a page that prints no surveys and no campaign.

## Privacy

The Personal Data Protection Act was not section-read. The hub publishes no roll, no photograph and no national identity number. Candidate names will be added only from a Commission notice that can be linked. None was opened on 27 October 2026.

## Verdict

Publish the hub and the election pages. No municipality pages. No survey. No count before 00:00 on 29 November 2026, Taiwan time. The same-origin file `public/live/tw-2026-local.json` stays empty until then.
