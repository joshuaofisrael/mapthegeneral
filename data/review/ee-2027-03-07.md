# Estonia legal check, 27 October 2026

This note is not legal advice. It is the editorial check for a national hub and the Riigikogu election of 7 March 2027. Germany, France, Japan, South Korea and Türkiye stay off the site.

## Date and hours

Read on 27 October 2026:

- National Electoral Service, Riigikogu elections 2027 (`https://www.valimised.ee/et/riigikogu-valimised-2027`). Election day is 7 March 2027. The advance week starts Monday 1 March and has six advance days. Electronic voting runs from 1 March through 6 March. Voting at foreign missions is 20–25 February. A postal-vote request must reach the mission by 5 February 2027. A candidate must be 21 by the last day of registration, 21 January 2027. A party must be on the register by 7 December 2026. That page does not print the opening or closing clock.
- Riigikogu Election Act, `https://www.riigiteataja.ee/et/akt/RKVS`, § 38. On election day voting starts at 9.00 and ends at 20.00. Subsection 4 lets a voter finish within 15 minutes after that end, and after the end of electronic voting. The count on this site stays off until 20:15, Eastern European Time (UTC+2), on 7 March 2027. Daylight-saving time in 2027 begins on the last Sunday of March, which is after this election day.

## Surveys

Read on 27 October 2026: `https://www.valimised.ee/et/valimiste-meelespea/valimisagitatsioon-ja-kusitlused`. Campaigning is forbidden inside the voting room. The same page says conducting public-opinion polls during the election period and on election day is not forbidden. A search of the Act text that was opened did not find a poll-publication ban. This site still publishes no poll and no projection. That is a choice of the site, not a statute copied as a blackout.
