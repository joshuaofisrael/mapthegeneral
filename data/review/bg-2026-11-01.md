# Bulgaria legal check, 27 October 2026

This note is not legal advice. It is the editorial check for a national hub and one election page about a possible new election for president and vice president on 1 November 2026. Germany, France, Japan, South Korea and Türkiye stay off the site.

## Silence and surveys

Read on 27 October 2026:

- Central Election Commission, voter question 21 (`https://www.cik.bg/bg/pvr2026/faq/voters/q21`). The answer says results of opinion surveys may not be announced in any form from 00:00 on 24 October until the end of election day is declared. Those dates are the first round.
- Pravko reprint of Election Code article 205 (`https://www.pravko.bg/law/c4627863-f8dc-4def-bbc8-748a486cd4a9?article=205`). Paragraph 5 says survey results may not be announced in any form from 24 hours before election day until the end of election day is declared on the territory of the country. lex.bg returned 403, so the consolidated code on the official portal was not opened. The citation is the reprint plus the Commission’s own answer.
- Commission Decision No. 231-PVR of 21 September 2026 (`https://www.cik.bg/bg/decisions/231/2026-09-21`). It cites articles 202 to 205. Point 6 says exit-survey results are announced no earlier than 20:00 on election day. Point 7 says they are not announced before the survey ends and before the Commission declares the end of election day.

Applied to 1 November 2026, the 24-hour ban runs from 00:00 on 31 October. This site prints no survey figures on any day. The count stays hidden until 21:00 Bulgaria time, which is the last hour article 220 allows for voters already in the queue.

A separate campaign-silence article was not read section by section. Complaints on the Commission’s list cite articles 182 and 183. That is a gap. The page still carries no campaign material.

## Hours

- Commission question 3 for this election (`https://www.cik.bg/bg/pvr2026/faq/voters/q03`): voting starts at 7:00 and ends at 20:00.
- Magistrat reprint of article 220 (`https://magistrat.bg/laws/00af9385cb23:220`): the same hours, and voting after 20:00 only for voters who are outside the room at 20:00 and hand over their papers, not later than 21:00. Abroad, 7:00 to 20:00 local time. The State Gazette text was not opened.

## Publisher

The site is an unofficial factual guide. It does not encourage a vote for a candidate. No Bulgarian media statute was read article by article. The survey-identification rules are not triggered, because no survey is published. That unread statute is a gap, not a reason to skip a page that prints no surveys and no campaign.

## Privacy

Bulgaria is in the European Union, so the GDPR applies to personal data. The Personal Data Protection Act was not section-checked. The hub publishes no roll, no photograph and no national identity number. Candidate names will be added only from a Commission determination. None was on the decision titles read on 27 October 2026.

## What was not clear, and what was still published

Decisions No. 405-PVR (23 October 2026) and No. 415-PVR (26 October 2026), listed on `https://www.cik.bg/bg/pvr2026`, still describe 1 November 2026 as a possible new election. Question 26 says the Commission names the two lists and schedules a runoff if nobody is elected. Those titles do not name the two lists or a ballot order. The page states that gap and leaves the candidate list empty. It does not copy district totals or news-report totals. National figures stay on `https://results.cik.bg/`.

## Verdict

Publish the hub and the election page. No municipality pages. No survey. No count before 21:00 Bulgaria time on 1 November 2026. The same-origin file `public/live/bg-2026-11-01.json` stays empty until then.
