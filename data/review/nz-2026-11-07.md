# New Zealand general election, 7 November 2026

Read on 27 October 2026. This is a source note for the page, not legal advice.

## Verdict

Publish an English national hub and one election page. Do not print party or candidate names. Do not print a count before 7:00 pm New Zealand daylight time on 7 November 2026. Do not print polls or a forecast.

## What was read

- Electoral Commission, Election Service Plan (PDF), https://elections.nz/assets/2026-General-Election/EC0131_Election_Service_Plan_A4.pdf — election day Saturday 7 November 2026; voting places 9am to 7pm; advance voting Monday 26 October to Friday 6 November with varying hours; overseas voting 21 October to 7pm on 7 November New Zealand time for people enrolled by midnight 25 October New Zealand time; preliminary results from 7pm on electionresults.govt.nz; regulated period begins 7 August and ends 6 November, with signs down by midnight; Whare pōti Māori voting services in te reo Māori and English; Māori Electoral Option is the Māori roll or the general roll; material in English, te reo Māori, and 29 other languages; freephone 0800 36 76 56; vote.nz for voting-place locations.
- Electoral Act 1993, latest consolidation, https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/1993/87/en/latest/ — section 3A (an election advertisement encourages or persuades a vote for a type of candidate or party; editorial content of a news-media internet site is a separate exclusion, and this site does not rely on it) and section 197(1)(d), (e) and (g). The 1 January 2026 PDF reprint was also retrieved: https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/1993/87/en/2026-01-01.pdf

## Rules applied

- The page does not encourage or persuade a vote, so it is not treated as an election advertisement under section 3A. The regulated period is open on the day this note was written. Spending limits on election advertising are not applied to this page.
- Section 197(1)(d) and (e) bar the stated opinion polls before the close of the poll. This site prints none, on any day.
- Section 197(1)(g) bars publishing a party name, emblem, slogan or logo on polling day before the close, with provisos for a newspaper published before 6pm the day before, fixed headquarters signs, and party names in news in a newspaper, periodical, or a Broadcasting Act broadcaster. A static page that remains online on polling day is not one of those provisos. Party names are left off the HTML.
- The tracker opens at 19:00 on 7 November 2026, the close named in the service plan. The same-origin file starts empty. 7 November 2026 is New Zealand daylight time (UTC+13).
- The Privacy Act 2020 was not section-read. The page publishes no roll and no voter details.
