## Rules applied

- The page does not encourage or persuade a vote, so it is not treated as an election advertisement under section 3A. The regulated period is open on the day this note was written. Spending limits on election advertising are not applied to this page.
- Section 197(1)(d) and (e) bar the stated opinion polls before the close of the poll. This site prints none, on any day.
- Section 197(1)(g) bars publishing a party name, emblem, slogan or logo on polling day before the close, with provisos for a newspaper published before 6pm the day before, fixed headquarters signs, and party names in news in a newspaper, periodical, or a Broadcasting Act broadcaster. Section 197(2A) is a defence when the name was placed on a website before polling day, the site is not pushed that day to people who did not choose to open it, and the site is not advertised that day. Party names were placed on 27 October 2026. Electorate candidate names, with the party printed beside them in the Commission’s spreadsheet, were placed on 28 October 2026. The freeze below is what keeps the site from being pushed or advertised on polling day.
- The tracker opens at 19:00 on 7 November 2026, the close named in the service plan. The same-origin file starts empty. 7 November 2026 is New Zealand daylight time (UTC+13).
- The Privacy Act 2020 was not section-read. The page publishes no roll and no voter details.

## Freeze from 7 November 2026

This is an editorial freeze on top of section 197, not a change to the statutory rule. The sealed baseline is `scripts/check-nz-freeze.ts`.
