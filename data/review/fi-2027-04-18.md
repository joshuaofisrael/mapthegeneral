# Finland legal check, 27 October 2026

This note is not legal advice. It is the editorial check for a national hub and the parliamentary election of 18 April 2027. Germany, France, Japan, South Korea and Türkiye stay off the site.

## Date and hours

Read on 27 October 2026:

- Ministry of Justice letter of 16 September 2026, VN/12981/2026-OM-3, “Vuoden 2027 eduskuntavaalit: Kuntien valmistavat toimenpiteet”. The copy opened is the PDF in the municipality of Loviisa’s notice archive (`https://loviisa.oncloudos.com/fi/kuulutus/202681054.80020.PDF`). vaalit.fi returned 403, so that site was not read. The letter says the parliamentary election is on Sunday 18 April 2027. Advance voting in Finland is 7–13 April 2027, and abroad 7–10 April 2027. On a Finnish ship, advance voting abroad may start on Wednesday 31 March 2027. Candidate applications must reach the electoral district board before 16:00 on 9 March 2027. The letter’s timetable prints election-day voting as 18 April, 09:00–20:00, and the preliminary count in the polling boards from 20:00. For advance stations, the letter quotes Election Act section 48: not open on a weekday before 08:00 or after 20:00, and not on Saturday or Sunday before 09:00 or after 18:00.
- The same letter says a government bill to amend the Election Act and the election-funding act was being prepared, to be given to Parliament in October 2026, with the changes intended to take effect on 1 January 2027. That bill is not treated as law on this page.
- Finlex returned a page shell for the Election Act. The Act was not section-read. The clock on this site follows the letter’s timetable, not an unread section. The letter does not name a time zone. This site uses Eastern European Summer Time (UTC+3) because 18 April 2027 is after the last Sunday of March, when daylight-saving time starts.
- The results service `https://tulospalvelu.vaalit.fi/` answered. Its front page did not print a 2027 count.

The Association of Finnish Local and Regional Authorities circular (`https://www.kuntaliitto.fi/yleiskirjeet/2026/vuoden-2027-eduskuntavaalit`) states the same day and the same advance dates, and says it repeats the 16 September 2026 letter. Outdoor advertising is recommended from 31 March 2027. That is a recommendation, not a legal blackout.

## Surveys

The letter does not print a ban on publishing polls. The Act was not section-read for one. This site still publishes no poll and no projection.

## Publisher and privacy

The page is an unofficial factual guide. It does not encourage a vote for a candidate. Finland is in the European Union, so the GDPR applies. The Data Protection Act was not section-read. The hub publishes no roll and no personal identity code.

## Candidates

No candidate list was opened. Applications are due before 16:00 on 9 March 2027. The candidate file stays empty.

## Verdict

Publish the hub and the election pages. No municipality pages. No poll. No projection. No count before 20:00 Eastern European Summer Time on 18 April 2027. The same-origin file `public/live/fi-2027-04-18.json` stays empty until then. 20:00 is the hour in the ministry timetable, not a section of the Act that was opened.
