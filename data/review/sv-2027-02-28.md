# El Salvador legal check, 27 October 2026

This note is not legal advice. It is the editorial check for a national hub and the elections of Sunday 28 February 2027. Germany, France, Japan, South Korea and Türkiye stay off the site.

## Date and hours

Read on 27 October 2026:

- Tribunal Supremo Electoral, press note of 10 December 2025 (`https://tse.gob.sv/publico/prensa/373`). The elections stay on 28 February 2027, in the country and abroad. The same day covers the presidency, legislative seats and municipal councils. Requests to register presidential and legislative candidacies run from 1 October to 19 November 2026. Municipal-council requests run from 9 to 19 November 2026.
- National electoral calendar (`https://tse.gob.sv/docs/calendarios/CALENDARIO-TSE-NACIONAL.pdf`). It prints the same election day for presidential, legislative and municipal elections. It does not print an opening or closing hour.
- Electoral Code PDF on the Tribunal’s site, file dated 12 May 2025 in the address (`https://tse.gob.sv/storage/documentos/20250512115356-c9f0f895fb98ab9159f51fd0297e236d-XhNHWzttAO.pdf`). Article 190 says voting begins at 07:00. Article 198 says voting is continuous and ends at 17:00. Article 189 says the electoral package is to be on hand by 05:00. No article read here adds time for a queue after 17:00.

The count on this site stays off until 17:00, El Salvador time (UTC−6), on 28 February 2027.

## Silence, surveys and projections

Article 176 of that Code says that from fifteen days before election day until the results are declared firm, parties, coalitions, natural or legal persons, and associations may not publish or broadcast survey results or projections that indicate a trend. The calendar applies that rule as a last publication day of 12 February 2027 and a prohibition from 13 February 2027 through 1 April 2027.

This site prints no survey and no projection on any day, including after 17:00 and through 1 April 2027. An official preliminary figure is a different act. Article 209 and calendar item 164 place transmission of preliminary results on election day. A figure may appear here after 17:00 only when it is copied from the Tribunal. It is not a projection.

## Publisher

Article 176 already covers any natural or legal person. No separate media-licensing statute was read article by article. That unread statute is a gap. The page is an unofficial factual guide and it does not encourage a vote for a candidate.

## Privacy

The personal-data statute was not section-read. El Salvador is outside the European Union, so this note does not apply the GDPR. The hub publishes no roll, no photograph and no identity number.

## Candidates

The press note’s registration window for the presidency and legislative seats was still open on 27 October 2026. No completed official list was opened. The candidate file stays empty.

## Verdict

Publish the hub and the election pages. No municipality pages. No survey. No projection. No count before 17:00 El Salvador time on 28 February 2027. The same-origin file `public/live/sv-2027-02-28.json` stays empty until then.
