Editorial preview

Last updated: 27 October 2026. Map the General Editorial Team.

What is at stake on Sunday 28 February 2027 is a federal popular vote on five proposals, not an election of the National Council and not an election of the Council of States. The Federal Chancellery release of 28 October 2026, read on 12 November 2026 in German, French and Italian, says the Federal Council decided at its meeting of 28 October 2026 to put those five proposals to a vote on 28 February 2027. The Chancellery’s English page for the same release, read the same day, says the content is not available in English. This page therefore uses the three language versions that were published. It does not recommend a yes or a no on any proposal.

The titles, as printed in the German release, are these. A popular initiative, Ja zum Importverbot für Stopfleber (Stopfleber-Initiative), BBl 2026 2712. A popular initiative, Für ein modernes Bürgerrecht (Demokratie-Initiative), BBl 2026 2714. A federal decree of 19 June 2026 approving an economic partnership agreement between the EFTA states and Malaysia, printed in the German release as Wirtschafts-partnerschaftsabkommen, BBl 2026 1777. An amendment of 19 June 2026 of the Nuclear Energy Act (KEG), BBl 2026 1772. An amendment of 19 June 2026 of the federal act on declaring collective agreements generally binding, with the parenthetical on minimum wages below cantonal minimum wages, BBl 2026 1770. Those are the Chancellery’s titles. A committee’s recommendation, and a party’s recommendation, are texts those bodies publish. None was in the releases that were read, so none is summarised here.

The releases that were read name polling day and the five proposals. They do not print one national hour for opening the ballot boxes, and they do not print a deadline for sending the voting papers to voters. This page does not invent either figure. No count is shown here before midnight Central European Time at the start of 1 March 2027. That is this site’s own hold. The official results service named for this vote is the Federal Statistical Office page on popular votes. Cantonal and communal votes are separate. This preview does not name communal executives.

This popular vote does not fill seats in the Federal Assembly. The next National Council election already has a page on this site for 24 October 2027. The federal popular vote of 29 November 2026 also has its own page. Those are different events. This preview does not copy a candidate name for 2027, and it does not treat a party programme as a recommendation on the five proposals of 28 February 2027. A programme is the text a party publishes. This preview does not rewrite one and does not rank the parties.
