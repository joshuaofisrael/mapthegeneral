National Council constituencyZug

The National Council constituency is the canton of Zug (article 149 paragraph 3 of the Federal Constitution). The Federal Council release of 27 August 2025 names the National Council elections of 24 October 2027. The circular of 19 October 2022 names the ordinary full renewal of 22 October 2023.

Gap. The executive head (Gemeindepräsident, syndic, maire, or sindaco) is not printed. No name from a cantonal or communal directory is stored on this page.

Gap. The Canton of Zug page “Termine Gesamterneuerungswahlen im Kanton Zug”, read on 26 October 2026, lists 4 October 2026 and 6 October 2030 for the organs of the Einwohnergemeinden, as ballot-box elections, on the same line as the cantonal parliament and the government council.
