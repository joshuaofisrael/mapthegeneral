Valid from01.01.1905
National Council constituencyValais / Wallis

The National Council constituency is the canton of Valais / Wallis (article 149 paragraph 3 of the Federal Constitution). The Federal Council release of 27 August 2025 names the National Council elections of 24 October 2027. The circular of 19 October 2022 names the ordinary full renewal of 22 October 2023.

Gap. The canton of Valais publishes the presidents and vice-presidents of the communes on a cantonal page. Those names are not copied here. The page: vs.ch.

Gap. The Council of State decree of the canton of Valais of 27 March 2024 on the election of the communal authorities for the legislature 2025–2028, read on 25 October 2026, sets 13 October 2024 for the conseil municipal, with a majoritarian second round on 3 November 2024, and 10 November 2024 for a conseil général where the commune has one. It sets 10 November 2024 for the president and vice-president, with a second round on 24 November 2024. Where the conseil municipal itself needs a majoritarian second round, the decree moves that election to 24 November 2024 and a further round to 8 December 2024. The historized commune register does not say which of these dates applied here. No later communal polling day is printed here.
