Aesch (LU)

Luzern · Wahlkreis Hochdorf

BFS1021
Valid from01.01.2013
National Council constituencyLuzern

The National Council constituency is the canton of Luzern (article 149 paragraph 3 of the Federal Constitution). The Federal Council release of 27 August 2025 names the National Council elections of 24 October 2027. The circular of 19 October 2022 names the ordinary full renewal of 22 October 2023.

Gap. The municipal executive is the Gemeinderat or the Stadtrat. The canton page calls these communal elections and points readers to their own commune. No name is printed.

Gap. The Canton of Lucerne page “Kommunalwahlen 2024”, read on 25 October 2026, states that the renewal of the municipal authorities and municipal parliaments for the term 2024–2028 took place on 28 April 2024. No later communal polling day is printed here.
