East Midlands

East Midlands Combined County Authority (EMCCA) is a Mayoral Strategic Authority.

Name on the factsheetEast Midlands Combined County Authority (EMCCA)
ONS codeE47000013
Current mayorThe GOV.UK factsheet read on 19 October 2026 does not name the current mayor. This page does not supply a name.

Elections

The factsheet says the most recent mayoral election was in 2024. It does not print the month.

The factsheet says the next mayoral election is scheduled for 2028. It does not print a day.

The GOV.UK timetable says elections for the mayor for East Midlands combined county authority are held in 2028, 2032 and every 4th year.

The English Devolution and Community Empowerment Act 2026 (Royal Assent 29 April 2026), Schedule 30, paragraphs 12 to 19, return combined authority and combined county authority mayors to the supplementary vote where there are three or more candidates. On 19 October 2026 legislation.gov.uk recorded those paragraphs in force from 18 June 2026 under S.I. 2026/656, regulation 2(b), with the savings in regulation 3. Before that, the Elections Act 2022, section 13, used the simple majority (first-past-the-post) system.

The factsheet says councils in the area have received an invitation to submit proposals for local government reorganisation. It says the most ambitious timetable is elections to new unitary authorities in May 2027, with those structures coming into being on 1 April 2028. This page does not treat that sentence as the next election of an existing council.
