North East Combined Authority (NECA) is an Established Mayoral Strategic Authority.

Name on the factsheetNorth East Combined Authority (NECA)

Current mayorThe GOV.UK factsheet read on 19 October 2026 does not name the current mayor. This page does not supply a name.

Elections

The factsheet says the most recent mayoral election was in May 2024.

The factsheet says the next mayoral election is scheduled for 2028. It does not print a day.

The GOV.UK timetable lists the North East among combined authorities whose mayors are elected in 2028, 2032 and every 4th year.

The factsheet says the mayoral election will be done by Supplementary Vote, as will further elections.
