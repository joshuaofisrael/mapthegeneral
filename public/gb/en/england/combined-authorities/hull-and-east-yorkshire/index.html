Hull and East Yorkshire

Hull and East Yorkshire Combined Authority (HEYCA) is a Mayoral Strategic Authority.

Name on the factsheetHull and East Yorkshire Combined Authority (HEYCA)
ONS codeE47000016
Current mayorThe GOV.UK factsheet read on 19 October 2026 does not name the current mayor. This page does not supply a name.

Elections

The factsheet says the most recent mayoral election was held in May 2025.

The factsheet says the next mayoral election is scheduled for 2029. It does not print a day.

The GOV.UK timetable lists this combined authority among mayors elected in 2025, 2029 and every 4th year. May 2025 is before 19 October 2026.

The factsheet says the mayoral election will be done by Supplementary Vote, as will further elections.

The timetable heading says elections for the mayors of these 2 combined authorities are held in 2025, 2029 and every 4th year. The list under that heading names three authorities: Cambridgeshire and Peterborough, Hull and East Yorkshire, and West of England.
