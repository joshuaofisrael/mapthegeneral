Greater London Authority

Greater London Authority (GLA) is an Established Mayoral Strategic Authority.

Name on the factsheetGreater London Authority (GLA)
ONS codeThe combined-authority file read for this page does not include the Greater London Authority. This page does not print a GSS code for it.
Current mayorThe GOV.UK factsheet read on 19 October 2026 does not name the current mayor. This page does not supply a name.

Elections

The factsheet says the most recent Mayor of London election was in May 2024.

The factsheet says the next Mayor of London election is scheduled for 2028. It does not print a day. The Electoral Commission schedule read on 17 October 2026 lists the Mayor of London and the London Assembly in May 2028 and does not print a day.

The GOV.UK local-election timetable read on 19 October 2026 does not put the Mayor of London in its combined-authority lists.

The factsheet says the Mayor of London election will be done by Supplementary Vote, as will further elections.

The factsheet says the London Assembly has 25 members, 14 of which represent constituencies and 11 represent all of London, and that the next Assembly elections take place in 2028. It does not name the members. This guide does not add Assembly constituency pages.
