Elections

The GOV.UK election timetable, updated 22 July 2025, says all 21 county councils hold whole-council elections in 2025, 2029 and every 4th year.

The next year that timetable text names after 19 October 2026 is 2029. The Electoral Commission schedule read on 17 October 2026 lists English local government in May 2029 and does not print a day.

The timetable says a Local Government Boundary Commission for England review can mean a council that normally elects by thirds elects the whole council, and that some councils may resolve to change their scheme.

Gap. The sources read for this page do not print the date of the last election held. This page does not supply one, and it does not reprint a result.
