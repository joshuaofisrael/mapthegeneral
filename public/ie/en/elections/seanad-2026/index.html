Seanad Éireann bye-election

Editorial preview

Last updated: 27 October 2026. Map the General Editorial Team.

What is at stake on Friday 6 November 2026 is one seat in Seanad Éireann, not a Dáil election and not a vote of the public. The Department of Housing, Local Government and Heritage said on 24 September 2026, in a release updated on 28 September, that Minister James Browne made a Polling Day Order for a Seanad bye-election. The vacancy arises from the election of Senator Seán Kyne to the Dáil. He was elected from the Nominating Bodies Sub-Panel of the Cultural and Educational Panel at the Seanad general election in 2025. An earlier version of the release named the Oireachtas Sub-Panel. The Department said that was incorrect. The seat to be filled is the one the corrected release describes.

How this bye-election works is unlike a general election. The Houses of the Oireachtas elections page, read on 27 October 2026, says the Returning Officer for Seanad elections is the Clerk of the Seanad. In a Seanad bye-election only members of Dáil Éireann and Seanad Éireann may vote. The public does not receive a ballot. The Department’s release says the last day for receiving nominations was Tuesday 6 October 2026 at 12 noon. The Seanad Returning Officer sat for the ruling on nominations on Friday 16 October 2026. Ballot papers were issued on Friday 23 October 2026. The polls close at 11 a.m. on Friday 6 November 2026.

The wider Seanad, on a general election, has a limited electorate that the Electoral Commission describes as graduates of named universities, members of the Dáil, outgoing members of the Seanad, and members of a county or city council. This bye-election is narrower still: the Department and the Oireachtas page limit the voters to members of the two houses. This preview does not list those members. A Dáil general election, which uses the single transferable vote and the public register, is a different event. No day for the next Dáil election was printed on the pages read.

The Seanad Returning Officer’s list of 16 October 2026 names, in the order printed, Ray McAdam, Caitlín Ní Bhroin, Maeve Maria O’Rourke and Patrick Paul Walsh. The list does not print a ballot number, a nominating body for each person, or a personal page, so this preview adds none. It does not attach a party label or a programme. A programme is the text a party or a candidate publishes. The public record used here is that list, the Department’s description of the vacancy, and the Oireachtas statement of who may vote.

This site prints no voting-intention figure and no count. The poll has not closed. A result will be added only from a count the Clerk of the Seanad or the Houses of the Oireachtas publish after 11 a.m. on 6 November 2026. Part V of the Electoral Reform Act 2022 has not been commenced as a rule applied to this website. The page is unofficial. Members who vote, and anyone checking the result, should use the Oireachtas page.

Who votes, and how

The Department’s release says the electorate is the members of Dáil Éireann and Seanad Éireann. It says the last day for receiving nominations was Tuesday 6 October 2026 at 12 noon. The Seanad Returning Officer sat for the ruling on nominations on Friday 16 October 2026. Ballot papers are issued on Friday 23 October 2026. The polls close at 11 a.m. on Friday 6 November 2026.

This is not a polling-station election for the public. A person who is not a member of the Dáil or the Seanad does not receive a ballot for this bye-election.
