Friday 6 November 2026 · Oireachtas electorate

Seanad Éireann bye-election

One vacancy on the Cultural and Educational Panel, Nominating Bodies Sub-Panel. Polls close at 11 a.m. Members of the Dáil and the Seanad vote. The public does not.

Last updated: 27 October 2026. Report an error

What is being filled

The press release of the Department of Housing, Local Government and Heritage, published on 24 September 2026 and updated on 28 September 2026, says Minister James Browne made a Polling Day Order for a Seanad bye-election. The vacancy arises from the election of Senator Seán Kyne to the Dáil. The release says Senator Kyne was elected from the Nominating Bodies Sub-Panel of the Cultural and Educational Panel at the Seanad general election in 2025. It says an earlier version of the release had named the Oireachtas Sub-Panel, and that this was incorrect.

The Houses of the Oireachtas elections page, read on 27 October 2026, says the Returning Officer for Seanad elections is the Clerk of the Seanad. It says that in a Seanad bye-election only members of Dáil Éireann and Seanad Éireann may vote. The public does not vote in this bye-election.

Who votes, and how

The Department’s release says the electorate is the members of Dáil Éireann and Seanad Éireann. It says the last day for receiving nominations was Tuesday 6 October 2026 at 12 noon. The Seanad Returning Officer sat for the ruling on nominations on Friday 16 October 2026. Ballot papers are issued on Friday 23 October 2026. The polls close at 11 a.m. on Friday 6 November 2026.

This is not a polling-station election for the public. A person who is not a member of the Dáil or the Seanad does not receive a ballot for this bye-election.

Candidates

The Oireachtas elections page, read on 27 October 2026, lists a file dated 16 October 2026 titled “Seanad bye-elections 2026 | List of candidates”, for the Cultural and Educational panel. The names in that file were not copied onto this page. The list stays on the Oireachtas elections page.

Result

The poll has not closed. This page shows no count and names no senator elected. A result will be added only from a count the Clerk of the Seanad or the Houses of the Oireachtas publish after 11 a.m. on 6 November 2026.
