Senate by-election in constituency 20

Lubusz Voivodeship. One Senate seat. Voting from 7:00 to 21:00.

Last updated: 25 October 2026. Report an error

What is elected

The President’s order of 25 September 2026 (Dziennik Ustaw 2026 item 1255) calls a Senate by-election in Lubusz Voivodeship, constituency 20: the counties of Krosno Odrzańskie, Świebodzin and Zielona Góra, and the city with county status Zielona Góra. The seat of the district commission is Zielona Góra. The order cites article 283 § 1 point 1 of the electoral code, together with the Marshal of the Senate’s decision of 27 August 2026 stating that the mandate of senator Mirosław Różański had expired. Article 283 § 1 point 1 is the death of a senator. Polling day is Sunday 22 November 2026.

Article 256: 100 senators are elected by majority. Article 260 § 1: Senate constituencies are single-member. The Commission’s constituency file, read on 25 October 2026, prints for number 20: 1 seat and 4 candidates.

How the seat is filled

Article 264: one candidate per committee in the constituency. Article 267: registered candidates go on the ballot in alphabetical order, with the committee name. Article 268 § 1: one “x” beside one surname. Article 273 § 1 counts as elected the candidate with the most valid votes. This page does not apply that rule to any name. Article 11 § 1 point 2: a Polish citizen with the right to vote who turns 30 by election day may stand.

Who can vote, and how

Article 10 § 1 point 1: a Polish citizen who turns 18 by polling day. Article 10 § 2 excludes a person deprived of public rights, deprived of electoral rights by the Tribunal of State, or fully incapacitated. Article 283 § 4: voting is only in Poland, and only for permanent residents of this constituency. Article 26: one roll, in the permanent polling district; the gmina prepares it. Article 39 § 2: 7:00 to 21:00, without a break. The order’s calendar prints the same hours.

That calendar, read on 24 October 2026 in the order’s PDF:

  • 9 October to 19 November 2026 — applications by voters permanently resident in the constituency for a certificate to vote where they will be staying, or to change the voting place, and applications to change the voting place from soldiers in basic service or on military exercises and from specified uniformed services housed in barracks;
  • by 9 November 2026 — notice of intention to vote by post from a voter with a disability, including a request for Braille overlays, and from a voter who turns 60 by polling day;
  • by 13 November 2026 — application for a voting proxy from a voter with a disability and from a voter who turns 60 by polling day;
  • by 12 November 2026 — the district commission’s notice of registered candidates;
  • 20 November 2026 at 24:00 — the campaign ends;
  • 22 November 2026, 7:00–21:00 — voting.

Article 107 § 1 forbids campaigning on polling day and for the 24 hours before it. Article 115 § 1 forbids publishing pre-election research results, and research results from polling day, during that same period. This page carries no campaign and prints no such results.
