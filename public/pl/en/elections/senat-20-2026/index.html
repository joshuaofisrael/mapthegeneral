Editorial preview

Last updated: 27 October 2026. Map the General Editorial Team.

What is at stake on Sunday 22 November 2026 is one Senate seat in constituency 20, not a renewal of the Sejm and not a renewal of the other ninety-nine Senate seats. The President’s order of 25 September 2026, Journal of Laws 2026 item 1255, calls the by-election in Lubusz Voivodeship for the counties of Krosno Odrzańskie, Świebodzin and Zielona Góra, and the city with county status Zielona Góra. The district commission sits in Zielona Góra. The order cites article 283 § 1 point 1 of the electoral code together with the Marshal of the Senate’s decision of 27 August 2026 that the mandate of senator Mirosław Różański had expired. That point of the article is the death of a senator.

How a Senate seat is filled, in the consolidated code announced as Journal of Laws 2026 item 1261, is a majority vote in a single-member constituency. Article 256 says one hundred senators are elected by majority. Article 260 § 1 says the constituencies are single-member. One committee may present one candidate. Registered names go on the ballot in alphabetical order with the committee name, and the statute counts as elected the candidate with the most valid votes. This preview does not apply that rule to any name. A Polish citizen with the right to vote who turns 30 by election day may stand. A Polish citizen who turns 18 by polling day may vote, subject to the exclusions in the code. Article 283 § 4 limits this by-election to permanent residents of the constituency, voting only in Poland.

The calendar attached to the order, read on 24 October 2026, is the timetable used here. Postal notice, a proxy application, and the district commission’s notice of registered candidates fall on the dates printed in that calendar, which runs through 12 November for the candidate notice. The campaign ends at 24:00 on 20 November. Voting on 22 November runs from 7:00 to 21:00. Article 107 § 1 forbids campaigning on polling day and for the twenty-four hours before it. Article 115 § 1 forbids publishing pre-election research results, and research results from polling day, during that same period. This page carries no campaign and prints no such results on any day.

What is elected

The seat of the district commission is Zielona Góra. How the seat is filled

Article 264: one candidate per committee in the constituency. Article 267: registered candidates go on the ballot in alphabetical order, with the committee name. Article 268 § 1: one “x” beside one surname. Article 273 § 1 counts as elected the candidate with the most valid votes. This page does not apply that rule to any name. Article 11 § 1 point 2: a Polish citizen with the right to vote who turns 30 by election day may stand.

Who can vote, and how

Article 10 § 1 point 1: a Polish citizen who turns 18 by polling day. Article 10 § 2 excludes a person deprived of public rights, deprived of electoral rights by the Tribunal of State, or fully incapacitated. Article 283 § 4: voting is only in Poland, and only for permanent residents of this constituency. Article 26: one roll, in the permanent polling district; the gmina prepares it. Article 39 § 2: 7:00 to 21:00, without a break. The order’s calendar prints the same hours.

That calendar, read on 24 October 2026 in the order’s PDF:

  • 9 October to 19 November 2026 — applications by voters permanently resident in the constituency for a certificate to vote where they will be staying, or to change the voting place, and applications to change the voting place from soldiers in basic service or on military exercises and from specified uniformed services housed in barracks;
  • by 9 November 2026 — notice of intention to vote by post from a voter with a disability, including a request for Braille overlays, and from a voter who turns 60 by polling day;
  • by 13 November 2026 — application for a voting proxy from a voter with a disability and from a voter who turns 60 by polling day;
  • by 12 November 2026 — the district commission’s notice of registered candidates;
  • 20 November 2026 at 24:00 — the campaign ends;
  • 22 November 2026, 7:00–21:00 — voting.

Article 107 § 1 forbids campaigning on polling day and for the 24 hours before it. Article 115 § 1 forbids publishing pre-election research results, and research results from polling day, during that same period. This page carries no campaign and prints no such results.
