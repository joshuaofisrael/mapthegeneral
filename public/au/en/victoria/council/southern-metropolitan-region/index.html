Southern Metropolitan Region

Victoria

Last updated: 18 October 2026. Report an error

The Victorian Electoral Commission state-regions page says there are 8 electoral regions in Victoria, that each region is made up of 11 districts, and that voters in a region elect 5 members of Parliament to represent them in Victoria’s Legislative Council.

Elections

The Commission’s report to Parliament says the Victorian State general election was held on 26 November 2022, and that on 26 November 2022 Victorians elected the 60th Parliament of Victoria.
