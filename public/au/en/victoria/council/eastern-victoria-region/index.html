Eastern Victoria Region

Victoria

Last updated: 18 October 2026. Report an error

The Victorian Electoral Commission state-regions page says there are 8 electoral regions in Victoria, that each region is made up of 11 districts, and that voters in a region elect 5 members of Parliament to represent them in Victoria’s Legislative Council.

Members

The Victorian Electoral Commission results by region for the 26 November 2022 state election print five members elected for Eastern Victoria Region, in order. Listed here are those members from that count who still held the seat on 18 October 2026:

  • 1st elected: HEATH, Renee, Liberal.
  • 2nd elected: McINTOSH, Tom, Australian Labor Party - Victorian Branch.
  • 3rd elected: BATH, Melina, The Nationals.
  • 4th elected: SHING, Harriet, Australian Labor Party - Victorian Branch.
  • 5th elected: BOURMAN, Jeff, Shooters, Fishers & Farmers Vic.

This page does not track changes after the 2022 count. The Parliament of Victoria publishes its current members.

Elections

The Commission’s report to Parliament says the Victorian State general election was held on 26 November 2022, and that on 26 November 2022 Victorians elected the 60th Parliament of Victoria.

The Commission says the next state election is on Saturday 28 November 2026. The Parliament of Victoria prints the same day and says nominations open on Wednesday 4 November 2026. On 18 October 2026 that day has not arrived. No candidate list is printed here. This page does not publish a poll, a projection, or a result call.
