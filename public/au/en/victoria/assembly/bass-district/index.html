Legislative Assembly district

Bass District

Victoria

Last updated: 18 October 2026. Report an error

The Victorian Electoral Commission state-districts page says there are 88 electoral districts in Victoria, and that voters in a district elect one member of Parliament to represent them in Victoria’s Legislative Assembly.

Member

The Victorian Electoral Commission results for the 26 November 2022 state election print the elected member as CRUGNALE, Jordan, Australian Labor Party - Victorian Branch.

This page does not track changes after the Commission’s last declared result for this district. The Parliament of Victoria publishes its current members.

Elections

The Commission’s report to Parliament says the Victorian State general election was held on 26 November 2022, and that on 26 November 2022 Victorians elected the 60th Parliament of Victoria.

The Commission says the next state election is on Saturday 28 November 2026. The Parliament of Victoria prints the same day and says nominations open on Wednesday 4 November 2026. On 18 October 2026 that day has not arrived. No candidate list is printed here. This page does not publish a poll, a projection, or a result call.
