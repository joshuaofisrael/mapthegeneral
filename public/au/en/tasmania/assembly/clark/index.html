House of Assembly

Clark

Clark, one of the five Tasmanian House of Assembly divisions.

Last updated: 19 October 2026. Report an error

The Tasmanian Electoral Commission says a general election for the House of Assembly was held on Saturday 19 July 2025, and that polling closed at 6 pm that day. It says seven members are elected for each of the five divisions, for a term of up to four years.

The names of the members elected for Clark were not in the results page retrieved on 19 October 2026. They are not filled in here. A copyright licence page for the Commission was not found that day, so no results table was copied.

The Commission’s page does not print the next House of Assembly polling day. This page does not publish a poll, a projection, or a result call.
