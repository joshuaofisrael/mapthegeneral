Saturday 28 November 2026

Victorian state election

The 61st Parliament: 88 Legislative Assembly members and 40 Legislative Council members. Voting on election day is from 8 am to 6 pm.

Last updated: 23 October 2026. Report an error

What is elected

The Victorian Electoral Commission’s state-elections page, read on 23 October 2026, says state elections are held on the last Saturday in November every 4 years, and that the Electoral Act 2002 (Vic) sets that date. It says the next state election is Saturday 28 November 2026. It says the Legislative Assembly has 88 members, one for each district, and the Legislative Council has 40 members, five for each region.

The Parliament of Victoria page, read the same day, says Victorians will elect the members for Victoria’s 61st Parliament. It prints the same day, 88 Legislative Assembly electorates, and eight Legislative Council regions with five members each. It says the current parliament expires on 3 November 2026.

Victoria · 88 Legislative Assembly districts · 8 Legislative Council regions

Electoral system

The Commission’s preferential-voting page, read on 23 October 2026, says amendments to the Electoral Act 2002 changed the rules, and that group voting tickets no longer exist for state upper-house elections. It says a formal lower-house vote numbers every box. It says a formal upper-house vote numbers at least five boxes above the line, or at least five boxes below the line. This page does not rank any candidate.

Who votes, and how

The state-elections page says a person must enrol and vote if they are 18 or older, an Australian citizen, and have lived in Victoria for longer than a month. It says the Commission may send a fine if an enrolled person does not vote.

The Commission’s 2026 page says enrolment must be correct by 8 pm on Tuesday 3 November. The state-elections timeline says the rolls close at 8 pm on the day the writs are issued, 3 November 2026. Nominations, on the 2026 page, open at 9 am on Wednesday 4 November and close at 12 noon on Monday 9 November for all candidates. The timeline says early voting opens on Wednesday 18 November and runs until 6 pm on the day before election day. The 2026 page says early voting is from Wednesday 18 to Friday 27 November, and that each district has a low-sensory mobile voting session on Tuesday 17 November from 10 am to 4 pm. On election day the timeline says any voting centre in Victoria is open from 8 am to 6 pm. Writs are returned on 19 December 2026.

The postal-voting page, read on 23 October 2026, says a postal application can be made from 9 am on Wednesday 4 November until 6 pm on Wednesday 25 November. A general postal voter applies before 8 pm on Tuesday 3 November. Ballot packs are posted from Wednesday 11 November. The page says there is no online voting. A postal vote is posted back, or left at a voting centre in Victoria, before 6 pm on Saturday 28 November, and must reach the Commission by Friday 4 December. The state-elections page also names interstate and overseas voting. Venue lists were not copied.
