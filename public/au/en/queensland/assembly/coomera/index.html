Coomera

Coomera, a Queensland Legislative Assembly electoral district. ABS State Electoral Division code 30022.

Last updated: 24 October 2026. Report an error

ABS State Electoral Division code 30022, name Coomera, in the ASGS2025 MapServer extract read on 24 October 2026. The Australian Bureau of Statistics says a State Electoral Division is a Mesh Block approximation of a state electoral district. It says those approximations do not match official legal boundaries and should only be used for statistical purposes.

The Electoral Commission of Queensland boundaries page, read on 24 October 2026, says Queensland is currently divided into 93 state electorates. The Commission’s 2024 state general election page says the election was held on Saturday 26 October 2024, and that all results have been declared. It says polling places were open from 8am to 6pm. This page does not reprint a count.

The Constitution of Queensland 2001, section 19B, says the normal polling day for an ordinary general election is the last Saturday in October in the fourth calendar year after the calendar year of the last ordinary general election, unless polling is postponed under that section.
